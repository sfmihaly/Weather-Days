    Data and method
    • Hs is the hourly significant wave height from Fisheries and Oceans Canada, Marine Environmental Data Section (MEDS), served through the CIOOS Pacific ERDDAP dataset DFO_MEDS_BUOYS. Where available the MEDS quality-controlled value (VCAR) is used, otherwise the buoy-reported value (VWH). Since mid-2022 only buoy-reported values exist and they have not been quality-controlled.
    • Values flagged erroneous or off-position, and values outside 0.2–20 m, are removed.
    • Hs is interpolated linearly between hourly samples across gaps of up to 2 h; longer gaps are treated as missing. Exceedances that start and end between two hourly readings are not seen.
    • Dates are in UTC. Cruise windows are limited to the March–November season analysed.
    • This is a planning aid based on a short record (from 2024), not a forecast.
